# DDS Creations: business plan (v1)
**Founder & owner:** Mathesh V · **Location:** Chennai, India · © Mathesh V. Confidential.

## One line
Career answers from people who actually hire: a trusted community connecting Indian job seekers with verified HR and hiring professionals.

## The problem
- Job seekers apply widely and get silent rejections. The people who know *why* (recruiters, HR) have no channel to tell them.
- Offer letters are confusing: CTC vs in-hand pay, notice buyouts, bonds, PF.
- "Pay a fee to get placed" scams target freshers, and there's no trusted place to check first.
- HR professionals have deep, valuable know-how but no public reputation and no way to earn from it outside their job.

## The solution: one community, four products
1. **Forum** (live): free Q&A with HR Pro badges, voting and accepted answers.
2. **ResumeCheck**: paid 1:1 resume reviews by verified recruiters, split 80/20 (reviewer/DDS Creations).
3. **Referral Circle**: reputation earned by answering questions unlocks genuine employee referrals.
4. **HireDesk**: low-cost employer subscription for SMEs to post verified jobs and reach engaged candidates.

## Why it can become very large
- **Flywheel:** each answered question becomes a searchable page, which brings in free search traffic, which brings new askers. More askers give HR Pros more reputation, which brings more HR Pros and better answers.
- **Two-sided trust:** verified HR identities are hard to copy, which is a moat that job boards and generic forums don't have.
- **Big, under-served market:** India's job seekers (freshers, tier-2/3 cities, career switchers) mostly can't afford coaching but will pay small amounts for specific help.
- **Expansion:** regional languages (Tamil, Hindi, Telugu…), campus partnerships, anonymised career/salary insights, then other emerging markets.

A billion-dollar outcome is a long-term possibility, not a promise. It needs years of community growth, a strong team, and eventually outside capital. The plan below gets there one step at a time without upfront investment.

## Revenue model
| Stream | Price (initial) | Who pays |
|---|---|---|
| DDS Creations Plus | ₹199/month | Job seekers who want priority answers and a monthly review |
| ResumeCheck | ₹299–₹999 per review (20% commission) | Job seekers |
| HireDesk | ₹2,999/month | SMEs and recruiters |
| Sponsored content / courses | Per campaign | EdTech, upskilling partners |

## Go-to-market (₹0 budget)
1. **Seed content:** Mathesh and 10–20 HR friends post and answer the 200 most common questions before launch.
2. **LinkedIn:** post one real (anonymised) forum question and answer daily, linking back to the thread.
3. **WhatsApp/Telegram job groups:** share scam-check answers, the most shareable content.
4. **Colleges:** free resume clinics run with placement cells in Chennai.
5. **Founding HR Pro programme:** the first 100 HR Pros get a lifetime 90% revenue share and a "Founding" badge.

## Milestones

## Tech (all free tier)
- Website and portfolio: static HTML on GitHub Pages with a custom domain
- Forum: Python (Flask) + SQLite on PythonAnywhere; move to a paid server and Postgres once traffic grows
- Next tech steps: email login, image uploads, payments (Razorpay), Tamil UI, a Java/Spring or FastAPI service for HireDesk
